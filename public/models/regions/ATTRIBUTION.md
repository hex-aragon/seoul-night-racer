# Original regional architecture

Created for Seoul / Drive in Blender 5.2 using `art/build_regional.py`.

Models: hanok (curved tiled roof and timber lattice), pavilion, glass-front café with terrace and parasols, lighthouse, pasture barn and coastal railway station.

These are original stylized architectural models, not scans or replicas of a particular building. No third-party meshes, photographs or textures are included. Generated geometry and materials are dedicated to [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Editable `.blend` sources and the reproducible script: [repository art directory](https://github.com/hex-aragon/seoul-night-racer/tree/main/art).

Higgsfield is not the source of these assets.
