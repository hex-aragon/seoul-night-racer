# Audio credits

## Recorded engine layers
- **engine-load.wav**: adapted from **Car Engine Loop 96kHz, 4s** by **qubodup**.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/car-engine-loop-96khz-4s
  Original file: engine-loop-1-normalized.wav in engine-loop.7z.
  License selected: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**.
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Modifications: mono conversion, 44.1 kHz resampling, filtering, loudness normalization,
  seam crossfade, variable playback rate and engine-load mixing in the game.
- **engine-idle.wav**: adapted from **racing car engine sound loops** by **domasx2**, loop_0.wav.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops
  License: **CC0 1.0** https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  The author states that this version was remade from a public-domain car recording.
  Modifications: mono resampling, filtering, normalization, seam crossfade and variable pitch.

These are recorded car-derived engine layers, not recordings of this Ferrari model.
No endorsement by the original authors is implied.

## Original music and effects
**Han River Afterglow**: original 104 BPM city-pop-inspired instrumental composed for this game.
Electric piano, bass, melody and percussion are synthesized; no commercial songs are sampled.
The composition and generated music are dedicated to CC0 1.0.
Tire friction/crash effects and the offline fallback engine are synthesized.
