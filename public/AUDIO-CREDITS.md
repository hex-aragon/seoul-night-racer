# Han River Afterglow
Original instrumental city-pop-inspired score, composed for Seoul Midnight Run.
104 BPM. Synthesized electric-piano chords, bass, original lead motif, and drums.
No sampled recordings, commercial songs, or external audio files are used.
The composition and generated audio are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Engine effects are synthesized from RPM-dependent combustion harmonics and gear-change envelopes. They are not recordings of a real Ferrari engine.
